Sin Acento Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Sin Acento is a language-learning app for practicing English and Spanish through lessons, pronunciation, and educational games.

Information we process

Sin Acento does not require users to create an account. Game progress, remaining lives, and cooldown times are stored locally on the user's device.

When a user purchases or restores Premium, Google Play processes the payment. Sin Acento uses RevenueCat to verify subscription status and provide Premium access. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and purchase history, subscription status, device and app information, and related diagnostic events for app functionality and subscription analytics.

How information is used

We use this information only to operate the app, remember local game state, verify and restore purchases, unlock Premium features, troubleshoot problems, and understand subscription performance.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Purchase processing and subscription verification involve Google Play and RevenueCat as service providers. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Security and retention

Data sent to service providers is encrypted in transit. Local game data remains on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed. Purchase records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as required to provide subscriptions and meet legal obligations.

Children

Sin Acento is an educational app for a general audience. It does not include targeted advertising or social features and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your choices

You can clear local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Google Play provides controls for managing and cancelling subscriptions. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at nextlevelsync.nls@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
